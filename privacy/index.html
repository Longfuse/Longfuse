LONGFUSE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Longfuse collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code in the app. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What the app stores

Everything Longfuse saves is written to its own container on your device:

  • Your progress: which boards you have cleared, your stars and best scores, and your charge balance.
  • A cache of the boards built for your device, so returning to one is instant.
  • A one-time random number used to generate your boards. It is not derived from your device, your account, or anything about you, and it is not shared with anyone. Its only purpose is to make sure your boards differ from everyone else's.

This data never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, this data is included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy rather than this one.

Network connections

Longfuse makes no network requests of its own.

The only network activity is Apple's own: when you open the store, view prices, make a purchase, or restore purchases, the App Store handles that transaction directly. Apple tells the app whether a purchase succeeded and which product it was for. We never see, receive, or store your payment details, your Apple Account, or any other identifier.

Data we do not collect

To be explicit, Longfuse does not collect or process: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, health data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal data. There is no advertising tracking, and the app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because it has no reason to.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data at all, it collects none from children either. It is suitable for all ages under COPPA, GDPR-K and equivalent regimes.

Your rights

There is no data of yours to access, correct, export or delete, because none is collected. To remove everything the app has stored locally, delete the app from your device.

Subscriptions and purchases

Longfuse Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Charges are consumable in-app purchases. All billing, renewal and cancellation is handled by Apple in your Apple Account settings. Use of the app is governed by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date above. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be to add a feature that does, and that would be announced in the app before it took effect.
